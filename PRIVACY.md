# Privacy Policy for LifeVault

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

LifeVault ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Philosophy
LifeVault is designed from the ground up as a **local-first, privacy-focused personal vault and memory archive**. Your data belongs exclusively to you:
- **No Third-Party Analytics / Tracking**: We do not embed ad trackers, telemetry SDKs, or data brokers.
- **On-Device Storage**: By default, all documents, reminders, dates, contacts, and personal information are stored and encrypted locally on your Android device.

---

### 2. Google Account & Google Drive Data
LifeVault offers optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive integration to give you full control over automated cloud backups:

- **What We Access**:
  - **Basic Profile Information** (email, profile): Used solely to display your name and profile photo on your device within the vault.
  - **Google Drive Access** (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): LifeVault only accesses, creates, edits, and deletes files that are directly created by LifeVault (such as encrypted backup archives). LifeVault **cannot** view, access, or modify any other files or folders in your personal Google Drive.
- **Data Sharing**:
  - LifeVault does **not** sell, share, or transfer your Google user data or files to any third party or remote server. All data communication occurs directly between your device and Google's official APIs via HTTPS.

---

### 3. Permissions Requested
- **Storage / Photos**: To allow you to import and attach documents, photos, or avatars into your vault.
- **Camera**: To allow document scanning and receipt capture.
- **Notifications / Alarms**: To schedule local reminders, anniversary alerts, and expiry notifications.

---

### 4. Data Security
- All sensitive records stored on your device use Android's encrypted private application sandbox.
- When backing up to Google Drive, vault files are exported directly to your private Google Drive app storage.

---

### 5. Data Deletion & Account Control
You have full ownership of your data at all times:
- You can remove your account, delete local backups, or disconnect Google Drive anytime in the **Settings** menu.
- You can revoke LifeVault's Google Drive access at any time through your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
