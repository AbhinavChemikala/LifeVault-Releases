Privacy Policy for LifeVault

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

LifeVault is designed as a local-first, privacy-focused personal vault and memory archive.

1. Core Principles

  • No advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data brokers.
  • All sensitive vault records are stored and encrypted locally on your Android device.

2. Google Account & Drive Integration

LifeVault optionally uses Google Sign-In and Google Drive for automated encrypted cloud backups.

  • Basic Profile: Only used locally on your device to display your display name and avatar.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): LifeVault only accesses, creates, and updates files specifically created by LifeVault. It cannot read or modify any of your other personal Google Drive files.
